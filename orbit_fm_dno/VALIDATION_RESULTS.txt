                                   (no DNO)  (+orbit)  (no DNO)  (+orbit)

  P3/perm-euclid            1.080     1.176     0.478    -0.302    -0.915
  C3/rot-head+prior         1.020     1.164     0.436    -0.287    -0.990

  Reading: with steering gain 0 the K rotations of an iid policy's source are K
  UNRELATED samples, so the search degenerates into best-of-K resampling -- which
  still helps a noisy objective.  The claim is the sharper one: at identical
  compute the coupling turns that blind draw into a structured 1-D search over the
  coordinate that matters, and the coupled policy ends up MORE conditionally
  accurate than the iid one despite starting less so.
